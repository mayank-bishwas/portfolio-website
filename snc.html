My Life runs on Sticky Notes

I make weekly sticky notes — for work, routine, life admin tasks, etc. — and rate myself at the end of the week, on the back of the same note. A form of journaling. Been doing it since Aug ‘24 — 2 years!!

I collated it all in Claude to get insights on myself for self-improvement. And, also, to make fun animation reel… like a year wrap :)
